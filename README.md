# SP-Photo-Print-Tool

Photo Print Layout Tool is a single-file web app for cyber cafes, CSC centres, Xerox shops and photo studios. Upload photos, crop and zoom, add an optional border, then auto-fill A4 or 4×6 sheets with passport-size grids. Set copies per photo, preview the sheet, and print. Works offline in any browser.
double-click Start Photo Print Tool.bat. You can also double-click index.html. It opens in your browser, and Chrome or Edge works best.

#Optional shortcut: Right-click index.html, then choose Send to, then Desktop (create shortcut).

#Daily workflow:
Drop photos into the upload box.
Click a photo in the list. Drag to position it and use the zoom slider to crop.
Choose the paper (A4 or 4×6 in), the orientation and the photo size.
Set the copies with the + / − counter, or click Fill the sheet with selected photo.
Switch the thin black border on or off, then click Print sheet.
Print dialog settings (important for exact sizes)
Paper size: match the app (A4, or 4×6 in).
Margins: None.
Scale: 100%, not “Fit to page”.
Headers and footers: Off.
Background graphics: On.

Print one test sheet on plain paper and measure a 35×45 mm photo with a ruler before a big job. If the size is off, check Scale and Margins first.

#Common problems
Photos cut at the edge: your printer has a hardware margin, so raise Sheet margin to 5–8 mm in the app.
Blank last page: set Scale to 100% and Margins to None.
Print button greyed out: add a photo and make sure its copies are above 0.

Your photos stay on your computer, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
