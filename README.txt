SOUVIK PHOTO PRINT LAYOUT TOOL - SETUP GUIDE (Windows)
================================================

No installation needed. No internet needed after you unzip it.

1. START THE APP
   - Right-click the zip file > "Extract All..." > Extract.
   - Open the extracted folder "PhotoPrintTool".
   - Double-click "Start Photo Print Tool.bat"
     (or double-click index.html directly).
   - It opens in your browser. Use Chrome or Edge for best results.
   - Tip: right-click index.html > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

2. MAKE A PRINT
   a. Drop photos into the upload box (or click it to browse).
   b. Click a photo in the list, then drag to move it and use the
      zoom slider to crop. Use "Rotate 90" if the photo is sideways.
   c. Choose paper (A4 or 4x6 in), orientation and photo size.
   d. Set copies with the + / - counter next to each photo, or click
      "Fill the sheet with selected photo".
   e. Turn "Thin black border" on or off as needed.
   f. Click "Print sheet".

3. PRINT DIALOG SETTINGS (important for exact size)
   - Printer: choose your photo/laser/inkjet printer.
   - Paper size: A4, or 4 x 6 in (must match the paper you chose in the app).
   - Margins: None.
   - Scale: 100% (NOT "Fit to page").
   - Headers and footers: OFF.
   - Background graphics: ON.
   - Quality: choose the best photo mode for your printer.

4. CHECK SIZE BEFORE A BIG JOB
   Print one test sheet on plain paper and measure a photo with a ruler.
   35x45 mm passport photos should measure exactly 35 x 45 mm.
   If sizes are off, check that Scale is 100% and Margins is None.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING
   - Print button is greyed out: add a photo and set copies above 0.
   - Photos look cut off at the edge: your printer may have a hardware
     margin. Increase "Sheet margin" in the app to 5-8 mm.
   - Photo is too big for the sheet: reduce photo size or margin.
   - Blank last page: make sure Scale is 100% and Margins is None.
   - Your photos never leave your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
